About

Small tools for making music feel more personal.

Strummer is an independent music-tool project operated by Adam Pang. The project joins listening, music-taste reflection, and hands-on practice without pretending that one service owns every part of a person's musical identity.

What works today

The Strummer player searches and streams music from Audius. Listeners can play trending tracks, search the catalog, keep lightweight liked-track references in their browser, and use the interface without creating a Strummer account.

Strummer Vibe is a separate Spotify-connected experience that turns authorized top tracks and artists into a taste profile and shareable visual card. Strummer Tune is a local browser tool for microphone-based tuning, chord practice, and analysis of audio files selected by the user.

What is still a preview

Wallet collection and full Spotify library import are not shipped transactions or import features. The player labels those ideas as concepts so visitors can distinguish available tools from the longer-term direction.

The public Strummer preview currently costs $0 and does not offer a paid plan, free trial, purchase, or performance guarantee.

Who operates Strummer

Adam Pang builds and operates Strummer. Project source and reproducible technical issues are available through the public GitHub repository. More information about Adam's work is available at adampang.com.
