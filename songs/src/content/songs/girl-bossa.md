### Five gates to ship

- [ ] **Finish the song:** capture the exact chords, riff, key, tempo, existing
  lyric fragments, and write the missing lyric sections and bridge.
- [ ] **Record the master:** lock the arrangement, record the human performance,
  mix it, and approve a lossless WAV or FLAC master.
- [ ] **Build the world:** choose the song's color palette and approve square
  cover art at 3000 by 3000 pixels.
- [ ] **Clear the identity:** confirm the Pacifica artist credit, legal
  songwriter names, collaborators, performer and producer credits, genre,
  explicit status, and whether this is a remaster or a new recording.
- [ ] **Release it:** Adam completes the DistroKid account, payment, terms, and
  submission, then adds the live store links here.
