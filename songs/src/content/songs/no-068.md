Fork of no. 067's study (Something, Harrison). Same technique, a fourth up,
your own melody: hold a major chord, let the bass walk down chromatically
underneath it instead of the chord itself moving. Chords and tempos aren't
copyrightable, the specific melody is, so this is a fresh melody over a
borrowed harmonic idea, same as every songwriter on both SONGWRITERS.md
rankings did.
