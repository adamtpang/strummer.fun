## The spec

- **Key** F major, 75bpm
- **Chords** F, C, C7, Bb, plain and diatonic, nothing borrowed
- **The move**: not the harmony, the form. A famously long coda (the "na
  na na" outro) extends the track well past where a normal pop song would
  end, repeating and building rather than fading a tight arrangement.

## Why this is the one worth stealing

Every other study this run is about a harmony choice. This one is a
reminder that the biggest move in a whole song can be structural: knowing
when NOT to end. Worth testing directly against your own habit of
2-minute sketches.
