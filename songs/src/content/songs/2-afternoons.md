## Lyrics

Approved by Adam on 2026-09-14. Verse 1 is Adam's, the whole of the
2026-09-12 Suno fragment (1:05). The chorus, verse 2, bridge, and outro are new,
drafted with Claude. Verse 2 carries the feeling rather than invented events, so
real details of the two afternoons can be swapped in later without rewriting the
song.

The shape: Adam's question opens and closes it. The chorus turns two afternoons
from a short time into something lasting. The bridge answers "so I made this
song for ya": this is that song, and it is as short as the time they had.
