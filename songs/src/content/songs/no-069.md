## The spec

- **Key** A major, ~104bpm
- **Chords** built on A, F#m, D, E, plus one borrowed chord: **D minor**,
  the iv from the parallel key of A minor, used instead of the expected
  D major
- **The move**: modal interchange. The song stays major almost the whole
  way, then borrows exactly one minor chord from its own parallel minor
  for a single moment of shadow before resolving home.

## Why this is the one worth stealing

Same family as no.067's chromatic bass and Template F's "let a minor chord
sit" (Kokomo etc), but a third distinct way to get the same ache: not a
walking bass, not lingering on a diatonic minor, but reaching outside the
key entirely for one borrowed chord. Three real songs, three different
techniques, same emotional target.
